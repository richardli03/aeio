---
title: a note on starting something new
date: 2026-11-15
---

i find i often get tired of trying to be truly creative.

i have many journals that have the first dozen pages filled with random musings and thoughts, but not much of it has escaped into the world. it leads me to feel deeply lonely sometimes, like these thoughts i possess in my brain, which i struggle and wrestle to get out, don't actually mean anything at all.

not that putting it on a little website is going to change any of this, but i feel there will be something deeply liberating when i post incomplete garbage on here or half-lyrics until things make sense.
